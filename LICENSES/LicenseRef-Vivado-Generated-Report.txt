Reports generated by AMD Vivado

The files this identifier covers (fpga/**/results/**) are reports that AMD Vivado generated while
building and analysing this project's designs: utilisation, timing, primitive counts and similar.
Their headers carry AMD's (and Xilinx's) copyright notices. They are kept in this repository for
engineering reference only, as a record of measured results. The Tanager project does not license
them, and they are not part of the hardware or software released under SHL-2.1 or Apache-2.0.
